    About this data

    NASA data

    NASA POWER

    Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources, NASA Langley Research Center. Monthly point values from MERRA-2 and POWER sources, v2.10. power.larc.nasa.gov

    Model

    MERRA-2

    NASA GMAO's Modern-Era Retrospective analysis for Research and Applications, one of the global models named in the challenge brief.

    Method

    Same tests as the atlas

    Day-weighted yearly and seasonal means (every month required), Sen's slope, Mann–Kendall with Hamed–Rao correction, Benjamini–Hochberg across the eight places, Pettitt change points.

    Retrieved from the NASA POWER monthly point API on . Each point is a model grid cell, not a weather station or a regional average.

      Reading it carefully

      A robust trend means the change is unlikely to be noise. It does not say why it happened. Gridded records smooth over local detail, and early decades rest on fewer observations, so cells with data in fewer than 70% of the chosen years are left blank.

      Trends are Sen's slopes with Mann–Kendall tests corrected for persistence (Hamed & Rao, 1998); the map controls the false discovery rate at 10% (Benjamini & Hochberg, 1995; Wilks, 2016); shifts are found with the Pettitt test. Regions average cells with cos(latitude) weights and skip years with under 80% coverage.
